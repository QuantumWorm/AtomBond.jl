AtomBond.rotate_view! — Method
rotate_view!(lscene, angle)

Turn the picture by angle degrees in the screen plane (counter-clockwise for a positive angle) about the line of sight: the viewing direction stays the same, only the up direction of the camera changes. Useful after a view along an axis, to put another axis horizontal or vertical.

AtomBond.view_direction — Method
view_direction(lattice, which) -> (direction, up)

Presets: :a, :b, :c look down a lattice vector; :astar, :bstar, :cstar look down a reciprocal vector, i.e. straight onto the (100), (010), (001) planes. A 3-vector is taken as a lattice direction [uvw].

source
AtomBond.draw_structure! — Method
draw_structure!(lscene, crystal, settings) -> (DisplayModel, plots, atom_plot)

Draw atoms, bonds and (optionally) the unit cell into an LScene. For a fully non-periodic structure (molecule) the box is drawn as dashed grey lines. Returns the plots so a caller can remove them and redraw without touching the camera, and the atom plot separately for picking.

source
AtomBond.DISPLAY_STYLES — Constant

Display styles, as in Materials Studio:

  • :ball_and_stick — spheres scaled from covalent radii, cylinder bonds
  • :cpk — space-filling spheres at van der Waals radii, no bonds
  • :stick — bonds only; atoms are joints the size of the bonds
  • :line — bonds as thin lines; only unbonded atoms get a dot
source
AtomBond.ViewSettings — Type
ViewSettings(; kwargs...)

Display settings of a viewer (v.settings), set by keyword, e.g. ViewSettings(style=:cpk, show_bonds=false). Change a field of an open viewer and call redraw!. Main fields: style (see DISPLAY_STYLES), ball_scale, bond_radius, show_bonds, bond_stubs, show_cell, show_boundary_atoms, wrap_atoms, show_labels, show_triad, orthographic, bond_lengths (:selected or :all), show_bond_angles, show_dihedrals, show_coordinates, show_charges, show_magmoms, bond_cutoffs (per element pair) and adaptive_detail (fewer sphere and bond segments for structures over 200 atoms while viewing; saved images always use the full sphere_resolution / bond_resolution).

AtomBond.open_library! — Method
open_library!(viewer, entry or name) -> Bool

Replace the structure with one from the library (undoable). It is Untitled until saved, so Save never writes into the library. box (vacuum in Å) puts a molecule into a periodic box for plane-wave codes (cubic box by default).

source
AtomBond.dos_figure — Method
dos_figure(r::DFTKResults; width=0.1, title="", dir=nothing, name="dos") -> (; figure, axis, width, save, ...)

A figure with the density of states of r (dos) against the energy relative to the Fermi level: the filled states (below the Fermi level) shaded, the Fermi level dashed, spin down mirrored below the axis for a spin-polarized run. A slider sets the Gaussian broadening (eV) and the plot follows it. Deep levels (semicore states, separated from the valence levels by an empty range of more than deep_gap eV, 5 by default) are left out of the view unless Show deep levels is on; the CSV always has them. With dir, a Save button writes name.dos.png and name.dos.csv there. The figure is not displayed; see plot_dos.

source

AtomBond.edit! — Method
edit!(f, viewer, what; reframe=false, keep_selection=false)

Replace the crystal with f(crystal) as one undoable step. what describes the step for the status line ("delete 2 atoms"). Errors from f are shown in the status line and leave the structure unchanged. reframe re-aims the camera (for changes of cell size); keep_selection keeps the selected atoms (for edits that do not renumber them).
