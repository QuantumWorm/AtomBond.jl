Labels and measurements

Selecting atoms measures them: 2 atoms give their distance, 3 the angle at the middle atom, 4 the dihedral, shown on the structure and in the line under the view. The View menu adds labels to the selected atoms, or to all atoms and bonds with Label all atoms and bonds (the same item switches back to Label selected atoms only).

Fewer selected atoms than listed show the plain measurement instead. Torsions through a straight angle are left out, and −180° is shown as 180°. Coordinates are fractional for crystals and Cartesian (Å) for molecules; Coordinates: use Cartesian / fractional switches. A copy of an atom on a cell face shows its own position (e.g. 1.000).

Charges and magnetic moments

AtomBond does not compute or guess charges; they come from the file, and atoms without one get no label.

  • Charges: CASTEP .castep output (Mulliken, or Hirshfeld when there is no Mulliken table), LAMMPS data (charge and full styles), extended XYZ (a charge, charges or initial_charges column) and PDB formal charges. Extended XYZ and LAMMPS data save them.
  • Moments: CASTEP .cell (SPIN=), CASTEP .castep output (Mulliken spins; one ħ/2 is 1 μB for g = 2) and extended XYZ (magmom, magmoms, initial_magmoms). They are saved to CASTEP .cell, extended XYZ, and by the VASP export as INCAR.spin with ISPIN = 2 and MAGMOM in POSCAR order.

Supercells repeat charges and moments; changing an atom's element clears them. Symmetry treats atoms with different moments or charges as different, so an antiferromagnet keeps its magnetic cell: AFM bcc Cr is Pm-3m with 2 atoms, not Im-3m with 1 (examples/structures/formats/Cr_afm.cell).

set_bond_lengths!(v, :all), set_bond_length_labels!, set_bond_angles!, set_dihedrals!, set_coordinates!, set_charge_labels!, set_magmom_labels!, or the corresponding ViewSettings fields, e.g. ViewSettings(bond_lengths=:all, show_bond_angles=true). For measurements on coordinates: distance, bond_angle, dihedral, min_image_distance.

Bonds

Two atoms are bonded when their distance is at most 1.15 × the sum of their covalent radii (at least the sum plus 0.3 Å, so H₂ and F₂ are bonded). Metal–metal bonds are left out when the structure also contains non-metals (otherwise Ti–Ti in TiO₂ would show as bonds); pure metals and alloys keep them. The limit can be set per element pair with ViewSettings.bond_cutoffs, or find_bonds(c; cutoffs).

A bond is also left out when another atom bonded to both ends sits between them (the angle at that atom is over 80°): an O put into a Si–Si bond gives Si–O–Si without the old Si–Si bond, and the bridging H atoms of diborane give no B–B bond. Three-membered rings (cyclopropane, oxirane), whose angles are near 60°, keep all their bonds. find_bonds(c; bridge_angle=nothing) keeps every bond within the distance limit.
